Ace High Poker — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Entertainment & Simulated Gambling Disclaimer: Ace High Poker is intended for an adult audience (17+) for entertainment and amusement purposes only. It does not offer "real money" gambling or an opportunity to win real money or physical prizes. Practice or success at social poker does not imply future success at real money gambling. Virtual chips and game items have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for real money.

1. Overview

Your privacy is important to us. Ace High Poker ("the App") is developed with privacy in mind. We do not sell your personal data, track your activity across third-party websites or apps, or display third-party tracking advertisements.

2. Information We Collect

  • Game Progress & Statistics: To enable gameplay, we store game state (virtual chip balance, player level, tournament rings, streak count, and table statistics). This is stored locally on your device and synchronized with our server to provide online gameplay.
  • Sign in with Apple (Optional): If you choose to link your account using Sign in with Apple, we receive an anonymous identifier provided by Apple to back up and restore your game progression across your devices. We do not collect or store your real email address if you choose Apple's private relay.
  • In-App Purchases: All purchases of virtual chips are processed directly and securely through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. We never have access to your payment card details, billing address, or financial information.

3. Data Usage

We use the minimal information collected solely to:

  • Provide real-time multiplayer card games and tournament matchmaking.
  • Maintain and synchronize your game bankroll, level, and accomplishments.
  • Process and credit purchased virtual chip packs.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. In accordance with Apple App Store guidelines, you can delete your account and all associated server and local data at any time directly within the app:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Tapping "Delete Account" immediately erases your profile, chip balances, and server records. This action is irreversible.

5. Children's Privacy

Ace High Poker is rated 17+ and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
