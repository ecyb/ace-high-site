Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-game Shop and tap the "Restore" button in the top right corner. Apple will verify your transaction history and restore any pending chip deliveries.

2. Can I cash out virtual chips for real money?

No. Ace High Poker is a social casino game designed exclusively for amusement and entertainment. Chips and game items have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money, goods, or services.

3. How do I transfer progress to another device?

Go to your Profile → Settings and tap "Sign in with Apple". When you log in on another iPhone with the same Apple ID, your progress, level, and chips will synchronize automatically.

4. How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → Settings and tap the red "Delete Account" button. Confirm the prompt to immediately and permanently delete your profile, chip balances, and server data.
